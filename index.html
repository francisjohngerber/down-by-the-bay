The Space

Salt air, slow mornings, and a braai on the balcony.

Down by the Bay is a relaxed, recently renovated two-bedroom apartment on the quiet Kabeljauws lagoon — made for couples, families, surfers and remote workers.

The open-plan living room opens onto a private top-floor balcony with a braai and lagoon views. There's a full kitchen, fast Wi-Fi, a clawfoot bath to soak in, secure parking, surfboard racks and even a little gym.

It's the laid-back, local side of J-Bay: swim, kayak or kite-surf on the lagoon, stroll to Pili Pili for lunch on the sand, and chase the famous Supertubes swell a short drive away.
